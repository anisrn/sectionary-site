Learn the sectional chart by touching it

A study app for the FAA Remote Pilot (small UAS) knowledge test, for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Chart questions are where many drone pilots lose points on the Part 107 test. Sectionary puts the actual FAA test figures under your finger instead of next to a list of letters.

Answer on the chart

Chart questions ask you to tap the right place on the FAA figure. Each tap is checked against hand-mapped areas of the chart.

Explore every symbol

Tap a tower, an airport, a magenta line or a big blue number to see what it is and what it means for a remote pilot.

See the airspace stack

Tap any spot to draw the airspace above it from the ground up, with floors and ceilings in MSL and AGL.

Also inside

Topic practice across the five ACS areas, a review list for missed questions, a timed 60-question mock test, a METAR and TAF decoding drill, the text of 14 CFR Part 107 and Part 89 for offline reading, and a test-date plan.

Sources. Chart figures come from the FAA Airman Knowledge Testing Supplement (FAA-CT-8080-2H). Questions and explanations are written for the app and cite 14 CFR, AC 107-2A, the FAA Remote Pilot Study Guide and the Aeronautical Information Manual. The figures date from 2018 and are for test practice only: not to scale and not for navigation.
