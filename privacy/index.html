Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · applies to the Sectionary app for iOS, iPadOS and Android

In short: your study data stays on your device. The app has no sign-in and no advertising and does not use analytics or tracking tools. The only outside service the app talks to is RevenueCat, and only to handle the optional full-unlock purchase.

What stays on your device

Your answers, your review list, mock test results, the test date you set, and which chart symbols you have opened. They are kept in the app's local storage on your device and are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them; you can also clear them in Settings → Reset study progress.

Purchases

The full unlock is an in-app purchase. Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your card or account details. To confirm and restore the purchase, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives:

DataWhy
A random app user ID created on your device (not your name or email)To link a purchase to this installation and restore it later
Purchase history for this app: product, date, price, store transaction IDTo check whether the full unlock is owned
Basic device and app information (OS version, app version, store country) and the IP address of the requestTo process and secure the request

This data is used only for purchase handling, not for advertising or tracking across apps. RevenueCat's own policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Ratings

After a completed mock test the app may show the system rating prompt (at most three times a year). The prompt is provided by Apple or Google; the app receives nothing back.

Links

Links to faa.gov, ecfr.gov and these pages open in your browser. Those sites have their own policies.

What we do not do

No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos access, no notifications, and no sale or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is a study aid for people preparing for the FAA test and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: privacy@aneesstudio.com.

Sectionary is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Federal Aviation Administration or any government agency.
